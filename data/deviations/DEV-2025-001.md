DOCUMENT_ID: DEV-2025-001
DOCUMENT_TYPE: Engineering Deviation
TITLE: Temporary Engineering Deviation - HV Battery Cable 95mm2
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Crimping
PRODUCT: HV Battery Cable 95mm2
STATUS: Expired

# Reason for Deviation

Temporary production controls were required following:

Electrical contact resistance increased from normal 0.18 mOhm to values between 0.38 and 0.46 mOhm.

# Temporary Conditions

Additional checks were introduced for:

crimp height, die wear, tool cycle count, conductor compression, electrical resistance

# Risk Consideration

Known historical cause:

Progressive wear of the upper crimping die increased crimp height and reduced conductor compression.

# Exit Criteria

Deviation may be closed after implementation of:

Replace crimp dies and reduce preventive maintenance interval from 50,000 to 30,000 cycles.
