# Reason for Deviation

Temporary production controls were required following:

Internal voids were found around the cable seal interface.

# Temporary Conditions

Additional checks were introduced for:

mold vent, injection pressure, material drying, mold temperature

# Risk Consideration

Known historical cause:

Mold vent contamination prevented complete air evacuation.

# Exit Criteria

Deviation may be closed after implementation of:

Increase mold vent cleaning frequency and monitor injection pressure.
