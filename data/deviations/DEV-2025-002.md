DOCUMENT_TYPE: Engineering Deviation
TITLE: Temporary Engineering Deviation - Copper Busbar Assembly
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Ultrasonic Welding
PRODUCT: Copper Busbar Assembly
STATUS: Expired

# Reason for Deviation

Temporary production controls were required following:

Weld resistance increased and weld energy trend shifted upward.

# Temporary Conditions

Additional checks were introduced for:

sonotrode condition, weld energy, weld force, surface contamination, weld count

# Risk Consideration

Known historical cause:

Sonotrode surface wear reduced energy transfer into the copper joint.

# Exit Criteria

Deviation may be closed after implementation of:

Replace sonotrode and introduce tool-condition checks based on weld count.
