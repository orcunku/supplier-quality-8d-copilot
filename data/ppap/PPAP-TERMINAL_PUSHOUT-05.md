# Lessons Learned

Historical risk reviewed:

Terminal moved backwards from the connector cavity during customer mating.

Known historical cause:

Secondary locking feature was not completely engaged during assembly.

Preventive control:

Add automated lock-presence verification and update operator work instruction.
