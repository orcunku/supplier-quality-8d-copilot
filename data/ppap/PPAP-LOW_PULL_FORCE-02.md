# Product Requirements

Product characteristics are controlled according to released drawing
and customer-specific requirements.

# Special Characteristics

Critical characteristics associated with this process include:

crimp height, applicator setup, pull force, operator setup approval

# Process Validation

Capability studies and inspection records demonstrated acceptable
performance at time of PPAP submission.

# Lessons Learned

Historical risk reviewed:

Cable pull force decreased below the 2.8 kN requirement.

Known historical cause:

Incorrect crimp height setting was introduced after an applicator change.

Preventive control:

Lock setup parameters and require first-off crimp height verification after tooling changes.
