# Special Characteristics

Critical characteristics associated with this process include:

# Process Validation

performance at time of PPAP submission.

# Lessons Learned

Historical risk reviewed:

Hole position exceeded drawing tolerance.

Known historical cause:

Progressive forming tool alignment drifted after extended production.

Preventive control:

Realign tool and introduce periodic dimensional capability checks.
