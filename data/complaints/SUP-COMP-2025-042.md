DOCUMENT_TYPE: Supplier Complaint
TITLE: Supplier Complaint - Connector Water Ingress After Thermal Cycling
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Complaint Description

Connector failed IP67 leakage verification after thermal cycling.

# Supplier Investigation

The supplier and internal quality team reviewed:

seal position, seal damage, insertion depth, housing condition, leak test

# Confirmed Cause

Seal damage occurred during manual insertion because insertion depth was not controlled.

# Supplier Corrective Action

Introduce insertion fixture and 100 percent visual seal-position verification.

# Verification

Subsequent lots were inspected before release.
