DOCUMENT_TYPE: Supplier Complaint
TITLE: Supplier Complaint - Void Detected in Cable Overmolding
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Overmolding
PRODUCT: HV Cable Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Complaint Description

Internal voids were found around the cable seal interface.

# Supplier Investigation

The supplier and internal quality team reviewed:

mold vent, injection pressure, material drying, mold temperature

# Confirmed Cause

Mold vent contamination prevented complete air evacuation.

# Supplier Corrective Action

Increase mold vent cleaning frequency and monitor injection pressure.

# Verification

Subsequent lots were inspected before release.
