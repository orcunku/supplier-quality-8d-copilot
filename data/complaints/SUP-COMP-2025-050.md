DOCUMENT_ID: SUP-COMP-2025-050
DOCUMENT_TYPE: Supplier Complaint
TITLE: Supplier Complaint - Leakage at Overmold Interface
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Overmolding
PRODUCT: HV Cable Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Complaint Description

Leak test detected pressure decay at cable-to-overmold interface.

# Supplier Investigation

The supplier and internal quality team reviewed:

surface contamination, cleaning method, adhesion, leak test

# Confirmed Cause

Cable surface contamination reduced adhesion to overmold material.

# Supplier Corrective Action

Introduce controlled surface cleaning and cleanliness verification.

# Verification

Subsequent lots were inspected before release.
