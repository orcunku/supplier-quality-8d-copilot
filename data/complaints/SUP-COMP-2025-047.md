# Complaint Description

Outer cable insulation showed cuts during final inspection.

# Supplier Investigation

The supplier and internal quality team reviewed:

fixture condition, insulation damage, cable routing, handling

# Confirmed Cause

Sharp edge on assembly fixture contacted cable insulation.

# Supplier Corrective Action

Rework fixture edge and introduce fixture-condition inspection.

# Verification

Subsequent lots were inspected before release.
