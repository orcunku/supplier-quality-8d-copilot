DOCUMENT_ID: NCR-2025-185
DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - Terminal Push-Out During Connector Mating
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Terminal moved backwards from the connector cavity during customer mating.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

terminal seating, secondary lock, insertion force, operator method

# Cause

Secondary locking feature was not completely engaged during assembly.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Add automated lock-presence verification and update operator work instruction.
