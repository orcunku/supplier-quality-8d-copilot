DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - Connector Water Ingress After Thermal Cycling
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Connector failed IP67 leakage verification after thermal cycling.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

seal position, seal damage, insertion depth, housing condition, leak test

# Cause

Seal damage occurred during manual insertion because insertion depth was not controlled.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Introduce insertion fixture and 100 percent visual seal-position verification.
