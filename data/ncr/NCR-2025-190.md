TITLE: NCR - Connector CPA Not Fully Engaged
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Connector position assurance feature remained partially open.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

CPA position, fixture travel, assembly force, visual inspection

# Cause

Assembly force was insufficient because fixture travel was incorrectly adjusted.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Correct fixture travel and add CPA vision inspection.
