DOCUMENT_ID: NCR-2025-188
DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - Terminal Plating Oxidation
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Incoming Material
PRODUCT: Copper HV Terminal
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Terminal contact surfaces showed discoloration and elevated contact resistance.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

plating thickness, oxidation, packaging, supplier lot, contact surface

# Cause

Packaging allowed moisture exposure during supplier transportation.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Improve moisture barrier packaging and tighten incoming inspection.
