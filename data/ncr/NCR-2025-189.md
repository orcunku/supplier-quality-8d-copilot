DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - False Electrical End-of-Line Failure
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Electrical Test
PRODUCT: HV Cable Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Good assemblies intermittently failed resistance testing.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

fixture contacts, micro-ohmmeter calibration, reference part, test leads

# Cause

Worn measurement fixture contacts added unstable resistance.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Replace fixture contacts and establish fixture resistance verification.
