DOCUMENT_ID: NCR-2025-187
DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - Copper Strand Damage During Stripping
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Wire Preparation
PRODUCT: HV Cable
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Broken copper strands were detected before terminal crimping.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

blade penetration, strand damage, blade replacement, conductor diameter

# Cause

Stripping blade penetration was too deep following blade replacement.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Add blade-position verification and first-piece microscopic inspection.
