DOCUMENT_ID: NCR-2025-182
DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - Low Pull Force After Terminal Crimping
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Crimping
PRODUCT: HV Cable 95mm2
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Cable pull force decreased below the 2.8 kN requirement.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

crimp height, applicator setup, pull force, operator setup approval

# Cause

Incorrect crimp height setting was introduced after an applicator change.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Lock setup parameters and require first-off crimp height verification after tooling changes.
