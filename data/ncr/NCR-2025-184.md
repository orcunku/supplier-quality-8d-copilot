DOCUMENT_ID: NCR-2025-184
DOCUMENT_TYPE: NCR
TITLE: NCR - High Resistance at Ultrasonic Weld Joint
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Ultrasonic Welding
PRODUCT: Copper Busbar Assembly
STATUS: Closed

# Nonconformance

Weld resistance increased and weld energy trend shifted upward.

# Immediate Containment

Suspect material was identified and isolated.

Additional inspection was introduced.

# Investigation

The investigation reviewed:

sonotrode condition, weld energy, weld force, surface contamination, weld count

# Cause

Sonotrode surface wear reduced energy transfer into the copper joint.

# Disposition

Affected product was inspected, reworked or scrapped according to
approved quality disposition.

# Follow-up Action

Replace sonotrode and introduce tool-condition checks based on weld count.
