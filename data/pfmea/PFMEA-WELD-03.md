DOCUMENT_TYPE: PFMEA
TITLE: Process Failure Mode and Effects Analysis - Ultrasonic Welding
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Ultrasonic Welding
PRODUCT: Copper Busbar Assembly
STATUS: Released

# Process Step

Ultrasonic Welding

# Potential Failure Mode

Weld resistance increased and weld energy trend shifted upward.

# Potential Effect

Product performance may fail specification or create customer
assembly concerns.

# Potential Cause

Sonotrode surface wear reduced energy transfer into the copper joint.

# Current Prevention Controls

Process setup verification, preventive maintenance and approved
work instructions.

# Current Detection Controls

Relevant checks include:

sonotrode condition, weld energy, weld force, surface contamination, weld count

# Recommended Action

Replace sonotrode and introduce tool-condition checks based on weld count.
