DOCUMENT_ID: PFMEA-CRIMP-04
DOCUMENT_TYPE: PFMEA
TITLE: Process Failure Mode and Effects Analysis - Crimping
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Crimping
PRODUCT: HV Battery Cable 95mm2
STATUS: Released

# Process Step

Crimping

# Potential Failure Mode

Electrical contact resistance increased from normal 0.18 mOhm to values between 0.38 and 0.46 mOhm.

# Potential Effect

Product performance may fail specification or create customer
assembly concerns.

# Potential Cause

Progressive wear of the upper crimping die increased crimp height and reduced conductor compression.

# Current Prevention Controls

Process setup verification, preventive maintenance and approved
work instructions.

# Current Detection Controls

Relevant checks include:

crimp height, die wear, tool cycle count, conductor compression, electrical resistance

# Recommended Action

Replace crimp dies and reduce preventive maintenance interval from 50,000 to 30,000 cycles.
