TITLE: Work Instruction - Ultrasonic Welding
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Ultrasonic Welding
PRODUCT: Copper Busbar Assembly
STATUS: Released

# Purpose

Define the approved method for the Ultrasonic Welding operation.

# Before Starting

Confirm correct product, tooling, program and inspection equipment.

# Process Checks

Operator must verify:

sonotrode condition, weld energy, weld force, surface contamination, weld count

# Abnormal Condition

If the following symptom is identified:

Weld resistance increased and weld energy trend shifted upward.

stop production and contact Quality.

# Known Risk

Historical investigation identified:

Sonotrode surface wear reduced energy transfer into the copper joint.

# Required Prevention

Replace sonotrode and introduce tool-condition checks based on weld count.
