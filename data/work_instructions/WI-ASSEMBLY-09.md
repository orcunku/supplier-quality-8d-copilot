TITLE: Work Instruction - Assembly
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector
STATUS: Released

# Purpose

Define the approved method for the Assembly operation.

# Before Starting

Confirm correct product, tooling, program and inspection equipment.

# Process Checks

Operator must verify:

terminal seating, secondary lock, insertion force, operator method

# Abnormal Condition

If the following symptom is identified:

Terminal moved backwards from the connector cavity during customer mating.

stop production and contact Quality.

# Known Risk

Historical investigation identified:

Secondary locking feature was not completely engaged during assembly.

# Required Prevention

Add automated lock-presence verification and update operator work instruction.
