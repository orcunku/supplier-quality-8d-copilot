TITLE: Work Instruction - Assembly
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Assembly
PRODUCT: HV Connector Assembly
STATUS: Released

# Purpose

Define the approved method for the Assembly operation.

# Before Starting

Confirm correct product, tooling, program and inspection equipment.

# Process Checks

Operator must verify:

seal position, seal damage, insertion depth, housing condition, leak test

# Abnormal Condition

If the following symptom is identified:

Connector failed IP67 leakage verification after thermal cycling.

stop production and contact Quality.

# Known Risk

Historical investigation identified:

Seal damage occurred during manual insertion because insertion depth was not controlled.

# Required Prevention

Introduce insertion fixture and 100 percent visual seal-position verification.
