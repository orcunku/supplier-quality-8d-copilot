TITLE: Work Instruction - Wire Preparation
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Wire Preparation
PRODUCT: HV Cable
STATUS: Released

# Purpose

Define the approved method for the Wire Preparation operation.

# Before Starting

Confirm correct product, tooling, program and inspection equipment.

# Process Checks

Operator must verify:

blade penetration, strand damage, blade replacement, conductor diameter

# Abnormal Condition

If the following symptom is identified:

Broken copper strands were detected before terminal crimping.

stop production and contact Quality.

# Known Risk

Historical investigation identified:

Stripping blade penetration was too deep following blade replacement.

# Required Prevention

Add blade-position verification and first-piece microscopic inspection.
