TITLE: Control Plan - Crimping
COMPANY: Anatolia E-Mobility Components
PROCESS: Crimping
PRODUCT: HV Battery Cable 95mm2
STATUS: Released

# Controlled Characteristics

The following characteristics require process control:

crimp height, die wear, tool cycle count, conductor compression, electrical resistance

# Inspection Method

Inspection is performed using approved gauges, process monitoring
equipment and documented visual standards.

# Reaction Plan

If a characteristic exceeds requirement:

1. Stop the affected process.
2. Segregate suspect production.
3. Inform Quality.
4. Perform containment inspection.
5. Investigate process change.

# Historical Lesson

Previous failure:

Electrical contact resistance increased from normal 0.18 mOhm to values between 0.38 and 0.46 mOhm.

Historical root cause:

Progressive wear of the upper crimping die increased crimp height and reduced conductor compression.
